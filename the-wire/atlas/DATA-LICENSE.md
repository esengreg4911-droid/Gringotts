# Data attribution

Public cast metadata and character-image source URLs were retrieved from TVmaze on 2026-10-31.

- Show: https://www.tvmaze.com/shows/179/the-wire
- API: https://api.tvmaze.com/shows/179/cast
- All five seasons’ episode guest credits: https://api.tvmaze.com/shows/179/episodes and each episode's `/guestcast` endpoint.
- TVmaze licensing: https://www.tvmaze.com/api#licensing

Adaptations include selecting and organizing characters across all five seasons, merging duplicate entries, correcting season-specific titles and child-actor assignments, and adding independently written Chinese descriptions, aliases, grouping and disclosure boundaries. Adapted TVmaze data in `data.js` is provided under CC BY-SA 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ .

Further factual references are listed inside the website. Character photographs remain subject to the rights of their respective copyright holders; the metadata license does not independently license HBO imagery.

## Supplemental portraits (2026-10-31)

32 character images were obtained from their corresponding The Wire Wiki character pages. Four additional first-season images were obtained from public Clip.Cafe stills/excerpts and used for five characters; Michael and Sean share a labelled group photograph. Exact character pages, file pages and original asset URLs are recorded in `data.js` and `PHOTOS.md`.

Photo preparation was limited to cropping and resizing genuine stills. No AI-generated faces or actor publicity headshots were substituted for character photographs. The character-image copyright remains with the relevant rights holders; Fandom's general text license does not license HBO screenshots.

## Five-season expansion and final review

The season-two through season-five supplements retain their original character-image URLs and provenance in `data.js` and `PHOTOS.md`. Dwight Tilghman’s added still comes from IMDb’s page explicitly identifying Antonio D. Charity as that character. This is a genuine production still; copyright remains with its rights holder. A candidate image for Grace Sampson was not downloaded because the source returned HTTP 403.

## Authorized local episode stills

22 additional character portraits were extracted from the user-provided local episodes, with source episode, timestamp, crop, and identity evidence recorded in `LOCAL-PORTRAITS.md` and `data.js`. Original videos were accessed read-only and were not modified or uploaded. These stills remain subject to the original rights holders.
